### Organizing collections

In the collections view, choose **New group** to create a group such as a city.
Choose its location to place it inside another group. The group menu also offers
**New nested group** and **New collection**. Use a collection’s **Move to group**
action to file it in a group, or choose **All collections** to return it to the top level.

Use **Edit group** to rename a group or move it to a different parent.
**Remove group** moves its collections and nested groups up one level and keeps
all collections. Groups can be expanded or collapsed. Search includes group names
and reveals matching collections inside their groups; ownership filters still apply.

Groups are personal, including when they contain collections shared with you.
Their names and structure sync across your signed-in devices, and cached groups
remain available to browse offline. Creating or editing groups requires a connection.
Group names are stored as ordinary account data; collection encryption and sharing
permissions are unchanged.

The organization API reads and replaces the caller’s group tree and collection
placements. Save with the revision returned by the latest read. A stale revision
returns HTTP 409; reload before retrying. Cycles, missing parent groups and duplicate
placements are rejected, and placements for inaccessible collections are discarded.
