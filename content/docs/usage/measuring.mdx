---
title: Measuring
description: Measure distances, circles and travel time, then keep the result in a canvas or a file.
---

Right-click the map, or use the tools menu, to open one of three measuring
tools:

- **Measure** draws a path and shows its length. Click the first point again
  to close the path into an area, and the area appears too.
- **Circle** shows the radius, circumference and area of a circle around a
  point.
- **Travel time** shades the area you can reach from a point within a set time
  on foot, by bike, by car or by transit.

## Keeping a measurement

A measurement is gone once you close its tool, unless you keep it. Each tool
has two buttons for that:

- **Save to canvas** makes a new canvas that holds the shape, then opens it.
  It measures exactly as it did in the tool, and you can label it, restyle it
  or draw more around it. A travel-time result becomes one shape per time
  band.
- **Export** saves the shape as a GeoJSON file. On a phone it opens the share
  sheet.

## Exporting a canvas

Choose **Export GeoJSON** in a canvas's menu to save everything drawn on it,
plus any data you imported into it, as one file. The file keeps each mark's
label, colour, icon and style. Importing it into a canvas brings the marks back
as editable marks rather than plain data, so a canvas can be copied or moved
this way.

The file also carries standard colour properties, so other GeoJSON tools such as
geojson.io show the marks in their colours.
