---
title: Measuring
description: Measure distances, circles and travel time, then keep the result in a canvas or export it as GeoJSON, KML, GPX or CSV.
---

Right-click the map (long-press on a phone) to open one of three measuring
tools:

- **Measure** draws a path and shows its length. Click the first point again
  to close the path into an area, and the area appears too.
- **Circle** shows the radius, circumference and area of a circle around a
  point.
- **Travel time** shades the area you can reach from a point, or that can
  reach it, within a set time on foot, by bike, by car or by transit.

Switch units with **km** and **mi** at the top of each panel, or tap a figure to
cycle its unit.

## Keeping a measurement

A measurement is gone once you close its tool, unless you keep it. Each tool
has two buttons for that:

- **Save to canvas** makes a new canvas that holds the shape, then opens it.
  It measures exactly as it did in the tool, and you can label it, restyle it
  or draw more around it. A travel-time result becomes one shape per time
  band.
- **Export** saves the shape as a file, in whichever format the place you're
  taking it to reads. On a phone it opens the share sheet.

## Export formats

| Format | Use it for | Comes back as marks |
|---|---|---|
| GeoJSON | Other mapping tools, such as QGIS or geojson.io | Yes |
| Google Earth (KML) | Google Earth and Google My Maps | Yes |
| GPS device (GPX) | Hiking and cycling apps and GPS units. Areas become closed tracks. | No, shapes only |
| Spreadsheet (CSV) | Excel, Sheets or a database. Points get latitude and longitude columns; lines and areas get a `wkt` column. | No, shapes only |

GeoJSON and KML files keep each mark's label, colour, icon and style, and
importing one into a canvas brings back editable marks. Other tools read the
colours too.

A spreadsheet you import can describe lines and areas the same way, in a `wkt`
or `geometry` column.

## Exporting a canvas

Choose **Export** in a canvas's menu, then a format, to save everything drawn
on it plus any data you imported into it as one file. That is also how to copy
a canvas or move it to another account.
