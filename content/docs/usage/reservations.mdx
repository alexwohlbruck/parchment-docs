---
title: Reservations
description: Book a table, compare hotel prices, or order delivery from a place's page.
---

Places you can book show a card at the top of the overview:

- **Book a table** for restaurants, cafés and bars that take reservations.
- **Compare prices** for hotels and other lodging, when hotel prices are set up on your server. Otherwise **Book a room**.
- **Book ahead** for other places that take reservations, like campsites or tours.

Pick the day, the number of people and, for a stay, the number of nights. The card starts at tonight, 2 people, 1 night. Every link opens with those filled in.

## Restaurants

When Parchment knows a restaurant's page on a booking site, the link goes straight to it:

| Site | Example page |
|---|---|
| OpenTable | `opentable.com/r/<restaurant>` |
| Resy | `resy.com/cities/<city>/venues/<restaurant>` |
| Tock | `exploretock.com/<restaurant>` |
| SevenRooms | `sevenrooms.com/reservations/<restaurant>` |

For a site where the page isn't known, the card searches that site for the restaurant: OpenTable everywhere, Resy in US cities. A general web search is offered only when nothing direct is known.

A booking page on any other site is listed under that site's name.

If the place delivers, the card also links to order from it on Uber Eats, DoorDash, Grubhub and similar services.

## Hotels

With hotel prices configured, **Compare prices** lists every seller of the room for your dates. Each row shows:

- the seller, marked **Official site** when it's the hotel's own
- the nightly price before taxes
- the total with taxes and fees
- whether it cancels free

Tap a row to book with that seller. **Book here** is the server's own booking partner. Turn on **Free cancellation only** to hide the rest.

Links to the hotel's pages on Booking.com, Expedia and Hotels.com sit underneath, and go straight to the hotel when its page is known. Google Hotels is always a search.

## Where the data comes from

A place's booking pages are kept per site, in this order of trust:

1. A choice someone made in **Business listings** (below). Nothing automatic replaces it.
2. The place's own OpenStreetMap tags: `reservation:website`, `website:booking`, `website:reservation` or `website:reservations`.
3. Its Wikidata item, for lodging: Booking.com hotel ID (P3607), Expedia hotel ID (P5651) and Hotels.com hotel ID (P3898).
4. Reservation links from Foursquare, when your server has premium place data.

Once a page is known it's stored for the place, so later visits, including on free plans, link straight to it.

The card appears for places tagged `reservation=yes`, `recommended` or `required`, for lodging, and for restaurants with a known booking page. Places tagged `reservation=no` show no booking links.

## Correcting a booking page

People with permission to edit listings see a **Business listings** section on the place page. For each booking site:

- **OpenTable, Resy, Tock, SevenRooms, Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com:** choose **Change**, then paste the place's page from that site. A search results page isn't accepted.
- **Google Hotels and LiteAPI:** choose **Change** and pick the hotel from the nearby list.
- **Not on …** says the site doesn't list the place, so it isn't searched there again.

A correction applies for everyone.

Every link opens in your browser. Parchment doesn't sign you in to these services or see your booking.
