---
title: Reservations
description: Book a table from a place's page when it takes reservations.
---

When a place takes bookings, its page shows a **Book a table** card at the top of the overview, with links to book it. Places that aren't restaurants, like campsites or tours, show **Book ahead** instead.

The line under the title says how much booking matters there: it takes reservations, recommends them, or requires them.

## Where the links go

| Link | Shown | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| Their website | The place lists its own booking page | That page |
| OpenTable | Restaurants, cafés and bars | An OpenTable search for the place's name around its location |
| Resy | Restaurants, cafés and bars in US cities | A Resy search for the name in that city |
| Search the web | Always | A web search for the name, its city and "reservations" |

When the place's own booking page is on OpenTable or Resy, that link replaces the search for the same service.

Every link opens in your browser. Parchment doesn't sign you in to these services or see your booking.

## Where the data comes from

The card appears for places tagged in OpenStreetMap with `reservation=yes`, `recommended` or `required`. Places tagged `reservation=no` show nothing. A place's own booking page comes from `reservation:website`, `website:booking`, `website:reservation` or `website:reservations`.

To add or fix this for a place, use **Suggest an edit** on its page.
