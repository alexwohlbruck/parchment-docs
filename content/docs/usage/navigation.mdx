---
title: Navigation
description: Turn-by-turn guidance for walking, cycling, driving and transit, one leg at a time.
---

Start any planned trip from its detail page and Parchment guides you along it: where to turn, how far is left, when to get off. Multi-stop trips are navigated one leg at a time. Arriving at a stop returns you to the trip with **Next leg** ready.

---

## On screen

| | |
|---|---|
| Banner | The next maneuver and its distance, the street, and what comes right after it. Before a turn on a road with painted turn arrows, a lane strip shows which lanes lead there |
| Bottom bar | Time and distance left, and the arrival time |
| Speed | Your speed, and the posted limit when driving |
| Road name | The street you're on, under your position |
| Hazards | A chip for what's coming up: crosswalks, railroad and uncontrolled crossings, sharp curves, stairs, steep grades |

The camera follows in **3D**, **heading up** or **north up**, set in the menu or under *Settings › Behavior › Navigation*. Pan the map and it waits; tap the camera button, or leave it for ten seconds, and it picks up again. With 3D on, the route and your position ride up onto bridge decks.

## Sound

The speaker button cycles **All**, **Alerts only** and **Silent**, remembered per way of travelling. Walkers default to alerts only. Alerts cover hazards, rerouting, transit stops and anything that needs you.

## Changing modes mid-trip

At each mode change, a card says what's next: park, lock or dock the bike, then walk 4 min to the café. It moves on by itself after a short countdown; tap **Not yet** to stay.

## Transit

Waiting at the stop, the card counts down to your departure. Once you're riding, it counts down the stops and says **Get off at the next stop**, then **Get off now**, aloud and with a buzz.

- **Missed it?** If you're still at the stop well after the departure, **Next departure** moves you onto the line's next run and re-times the rest of the trip. **Another way** plans afresh from where you are.
- **Missed your stop?** You'll be offered another way from where you are.

## Along the way

- **Search along route** finds gas, charging, coffee, restrooms and more ahead of you, with the time each detour adds. **Add stop** routes you there first, then continues as the next leg.
- **Routes** lists the ways to finish the current stretch. Parchment also suggests one on its own when it's clearly faster, or, when riding or walking, has more protected bike lanes or quieter streets.
- **Bike share** trips watch your station. If it's filling up or running out of bikes, you're offered a nearby one; when it's full or empty, Parchment switches for you (*Switch bike share stations*, a premium setting).

Leave the route and Parchment reroutes from where you're heading, rather than sending you back.
