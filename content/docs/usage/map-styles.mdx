---
title: Map styles
description: Choose how the map looks, from Parchment's own palette to styles in the spirit of Apple Maps and Google Maps.
---

Pick a map style under **Settings → Appearance → Map style**. Each style follows
the app's light or dark theme, and works with every map feature: 3D buildings,
street-level road detail, transit and the globe.

| Style | Looks like |
|---|---|
| **Parchment** | Warm paper tones, softly coloured buildings and Geist labels. The default. |
| **Cupertino** | Clean and neutral, in the spirit of Apple Maps. Pale land, grey highways, and streets that turn from white to grey asphalt as you zoom in. Labels are set in Inter, with road names in spaced capitals and neighbourhoods in tall, narrow Barlow Condensed. At night the map turns slate blue with deep teal parks. |
| **Mountain View** | In the spirit of Google Maps. Light grey land, white streets, blue-grey highways, mint parks and Roboto labels. At night the map turns navy, with slate-blue streets and indigo buildings. |

Shops and commercial districts get a light warm tint in every style, so busy
areas stand out at city zoom.

## Fonts

Every font is open-licensed and served with the app, so labels work offline and
on self-hosted servers. A style's fonts load only when it is selected.
