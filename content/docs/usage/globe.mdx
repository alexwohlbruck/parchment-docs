---
title: Globe view
description: Explore the globe with stars, sunlight, and city lights.
---

With the MapLibre map engine and Globe projection selected in Appearance, zoom
out to see Earth against a black sky with subtle stars and a blue atmosphere.
The sky follows the camera as you turn the globe, and the atmosphere follows its
edge when you tilt the map. A softly glowing sun appears at its real position
and disappears behind Earth when the globe blocks it.

The effect works with the standard, satellite, and hybrid basemaps, in either
app theme. It fades away as you zoom in so the usual map sky returns for closer
views.

![Globe with stars and a blue atmosphere](/images/globe-sky.png)

Turn on **Day & Night** in the layers selector to light the globe with the
current sun position. A soft twilight boundary separates day from night, the
atmosphere brightens on the sunlit side, and warm city lights appear after
sunset. The dark side keeps enough ambient light to see its terrain.

![Satellite globe with sunlight, twilight, and city lights](/images/globe-sky-satellite.png)

City lights use [NASA's 2016 Black Marble composite](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps/),
not live observations. A compressed global texture loads only when Day & Night
is enabled in globe view; it needs no external imagery service or account.

![Nighttime Earth and the sun](/images/globe-sun.png)
