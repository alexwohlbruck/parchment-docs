## First sign-in

Onboarding helps you set up your profile and secure your encrypted data:

1. **Your profile:** choose a name, username, and profile photo.
2. **Preferences:** set your language, units, accent color, and display mode.
3. **Recovery key:** copy your key into a password manager, then confirm that you have saved it. Keep it private.
4. **Passkey:** create a passkey, or enable recovery on one you already use. You can choose **Skip for now** and continue with your recovery key.
5. **OpenStreetMap:** [connect your account](integrations/openstreetmap-account) to contribute map notes and place updates, or skip this step. Onboarding continues automatically when the account connects.

Your server may also offer plan selection and integration setup.

A paper map unfolds as setup finishes. This animation is skipped when your device prefers reduced motion.

If you lose every device and passkey, your recovery key is your way back to your encrypted data.
