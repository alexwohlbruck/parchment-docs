Mapbox provides the base map tiles rendered in Parchment's map view. It is the primary map engine integration.

---

## Capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| **Map Engine** | Vector and raster tile rendering for the main map |
| **Indoor maps** | Floor plans for venues Mapbox has mapped indoors, with a floor selector |

---

## Indoor maps

Turn on **Settings → Appearance → Map configuration → Indoor maps** to render indoor
floor plans. A floor selector appears on the right edge of the map whenever a mapped
venue — an airport, mall or stadium — is in view; it stays hidden everywhere else.

Indoor rendering is experimental in Mapbox GL JS and comes with two limits:

- It only works on the **standard** basemap. On satellite or hybrid the setting has no effect.
- Floor plans start drawing at zoom 16.

Coverage is limited to venues Mapbox has surveyed, so most buildings show nothing.
Where a venue is only partly mapped, Mapbox's own style logs repeated
`floor_id evaluated to null` and `Missing source layer` warnings to the browser
console — these come from the Mapbox style, not Parchment, and are harmless.

---

## Configuration

In **Settings → Integrations**, click the settings icon on the Mapbox card.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **API Key** | Your Mapbox public access token. Found in your [Mapbox account dashboard](https://account.mapbox.com) under **Access tokens**. Tokens start with `pk.`. |

Click **Test Connection** before saving.

---

## Notes

Mapbox tiles are subject to [Mapbox's pricing](https://www.mapbox.com/pricing) based on map load volume. The free tier covers development and low-traffic deployments.
